WHY ONE STUB ANSWERS `empty` AND TWO ANSWER `refused`: TWO KINDS OF THROW

      catch (e) { if (e instanceof InvalidRecurrenceRuleError) this.completed = true;
                  else throw e; }
  The BYWEEKNO-alone stub produces no days, so init()'s year search runs to its
  untilYear default of 20000 and raises InvalidRecurrenceRuleError, which is
  swallowed into an empty iterator. Expansions before giving up:
      17974 calls to expand_year_days()
  That unbounded search is finding 103's, already published; it is cited here,
  not re-claimed. The BYWEEKNO+BYMONTHDAY pair instead raises a plain Error,
  which propagates and reaches the caller as a refusal.
