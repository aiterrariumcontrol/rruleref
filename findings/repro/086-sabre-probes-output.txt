A  nextWeekly() advances by ONE HOUR when the rule carries BYHOUR, and the
   week rollover it then looks for can only fire at hour 0 of the first day
   of the week -- a no-op at INTERVAL=1. So a weekly rule becomes a DAILY
   one: this asks for 9 o'clock once a week and gets 9 o'clock every day.
   Unlike every other defect in finding 076, this ADDS occurrences.
  RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY;BYHOUR=9   DTSTART:20260302T090000
  sabre : 20260302T090000 20260303T090000 20260304T090000 20260305T090000 20260306T090000 20260307T090000
  RFC   : 20260302T090000 20260309T090000 20260316T090000 20260323T090000 20260330T090000 20260406T090000

B  nextYearly()'s BYMONTH branch walks the month number and then calls
   setDate() with the day number it already had. PHP's setDate() overflows
   rather than rejecting, so 29 February in a common year becomes 1 March --
   and the day number for every later occurrence is then 1.
  RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;INTERVAL=2;BYMONTH=2   DTSTART:20240229T090000
  sabre : 20240229T090000 20260301T090000 20280201T090000 20300201T090000 20320201T090000 20340201T090000
  RFC   : 20240229T090000 20280229T090000 20320229T090000 20360229T090000 20400229T090000 20440229T090000

C  getMonthlyOccurrences() is called for ONE month and applies BYSETPOS
   inside it, so at FREQ=YEARLY the set BYSETPOS counts within is the month,
   not the year. 'the 2nd Wednesday of September-or-November' becomes 'the
   2nd Wednesday of September AND the 2nd Wednesday of November'.
