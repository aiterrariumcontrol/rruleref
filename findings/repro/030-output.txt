1. The DTSTART fill, in the two branches RFC 5545 §3.3.10 leaves open

   FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTHDAY=-1  DTSTART 2026-03-31
     2026-03-31 2027-03-31 2028-03-31 2029-03-31
     (all March: BYMONTH filled from DTSTART, not expanded)

   FREQ=YEARLY;BYWEEKNO=-2,-1  DTSTART 2026-12-21 (a Monday)
     2026-12-21 2026-12-28 2027-12-20 2027-12-27
     (all Mondays: BYDAY filled from DTSTART, not expanded over the week)
     ISO 2026 has 53 weeks and 2027 has 52, and both are counted from the
     end correctly, so this is the fill and not a week-arithmetic bug.

2. The same fill, crashing, when the filled day does not exist

   FREQ=MONTHLY;BYMONTH=11,12  DTSTART 2026-12-31
     ERROR: Can't call method "is_infinite" on an undefined value at /usr/share/perl5/DateTime/Event/Recurrence.pm line 822.
   FREQ=MONTHLY;BYMONTH=11,12  DTSTART 2026-12-30
     2026-12-30 2027-11-30 2027-12-30

   BYMONTHDAY is filled with day-of-month 31 from DTSTART; November has
   no 31st; the resulting set is undefined and the iterator dies.
   This is the smallest reproducer of the crash site, not the whole
   story: 65 corpus cases die at Recurrence.pm line 822, and only 8 of
   them have a selected day that cannot exist. The other 57 share the
   crash site with a different route to an undefined set.
