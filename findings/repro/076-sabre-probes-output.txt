   date is never advanced and the iterator emits DTSTART for ever.

C  The leap-day guard at the top of nextYearly() runs BEFORE BYYEARDAY is
   consulted, so the first occurrence that lands on 29 February pins the
   whole remaining series to 29 February. Day 60 is 1 March in a common
   year and 29 February in a leap year; after 2028 sabre never returns
   day 60 again.
  RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYYEARDAY=60   DTSTART:20260301T090000
  sabre : 20260301T090000 20270301T090000 20280229T090000 20320229T090000 20360229T090000 20400229T090000
  RFC   : 20260301T090000 20270301T090000 20280229T090000 20290301T090000 20300301T090000 20310301T090000

D  BYWEEKNO emits ONE day per week, not the week. The offset handed to
   setISODate() comes from $dayMap (SU=0..SA=6) but setISODate numbers
   MO=1..SU=7, and with no BYDAY the branch defaults to the bare offset 1.
  RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYWEEKNO=2   DTSTART:20260105T090000
  sabre : 20260105T090000 20270111T090000 20280110T090000 20290108T090000
  RFC   : 20260105T090000 20260106T090000 20260107T090000 20260108T090000

E  The same index mismatch at BYYEARDAY, where the offsets are compared
   against format('N'). BYDAY=SU is $dayMap 0 and format('N') never
   returns 0, so no day matches and the iterator walks years for ever.
   Shown with BYDAY=MO, which survives the mismatch by coincidence.
  RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYYEARDAY=100,200;BYDAY=MO   DTSTART:20260410T090000
  sabre : 20260410T090000 20270719T090000 20340410T090000 20380719T090000
  RFC   : (day 100 is 10 Apr, day 200 is 19 Jul; only the Mondays among them)
