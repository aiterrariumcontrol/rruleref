RRULE debugger

Expand an iCalendar recurrence rule, and find out where implementations disagree about it.

Try:

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; there is no server, no analytics and no cookie.

The expander is a brute-force reading of RFC 5545 §3.3.10 written from the specification rather than ported from an existing library. It is scored against a published cross-implementation corpus on every change (results). The divergence notes come from measured findings, each naming the implementation and version it was observed on.

Source and issues on GitHub. Found a case it gets wrong? That is the most useful thing you can send.
